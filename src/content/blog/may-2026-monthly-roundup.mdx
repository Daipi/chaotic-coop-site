## What's New This Month

May 2026 continues the strong trend we've seen since late 2025: co-op horror is no longer a niche. The success of games like `R.E.P.O.`, `Lethal Company`, and `Content Warning` has spawned an entire wave of physics-chaos and proximity-chat horror titles.

Here's what matters this month:

## R.E.P.O. Still Leads the Pack

`R.E.P.O.` remains the most recommended co-op horror game for friend groups. Its combination of physics-driven chaos, proximity chat, and support for up to 6 players makes it the easiest recommendation for most game nights.

If your group has not tried it yet, start with [`games like R.E.P.O.`](/games-like/repo/) to see the full landscape of alternatives.

## Phasmophobia Keeps Evolving

`Phasmophobia` continues to receive major updates in 2026, including map reworks and new ghost types. For groups that want investigation depth over pure chaos, it remains the strongest pick in the genre.

See [`R.E.P.O. vs Phasmophobia`](/features/repo-vs-phasmophobia/) for a direct comparison if your group is deciding between spectacle and depth.

## Lethal Company Holds Strong

Despite being one of the older titles in this wave, `Lethal Company` maintains a dedicated player base. Its tighter 1-4 player focus and cleaner salvage loop make it the best pick for smaller groups that want pure tension.

Read [`R.E.P.O. vs Lethal Company`](/features/repo-vs-lethal-company/) to decide which fits your group better.

## Emerging Titles to Watch

Several new indie titles have launched in the physics-chaos horror space. We're tracking these and adding them to our recommendations as they prove themselves. Check our [`best chaotic co-op games`](/best/chaotic-coop-games/) page for the latest additions.

## What to Play This Weekend

Based on group size and horror preference:

- **2-4 players, want pure tension**: [`Lethal Company`](/games-like/lethal-company/)
- **4-6 players, want chaos and laughs**: [`R.E.P.O.`](/games-like/repo/)
