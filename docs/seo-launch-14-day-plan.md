# CoopQueue 14-Day Launch Plan

## Goal

Get `CoopQueue` from "freshly live and well-structured" to:

- first meaningful indexing
- first impressions and clicks
- first real trust signals
- a cleaner path to monetization later

This plan assumes the site is already live at `https://coopqueue.com/`, `robots.txt` is open, and the sitemap is healthy.

## What Matters Right Now

Do not spend the next two weeks mass-producing new pages.

The current bottleneck is:

1. discovery
2. indexing
3. first external trust signals

The site already has enough core content to earn early rankings on narrower comparison and constrained-intent queries.

## Core Pages To Push First

Submit and monitor these first:

1. `/`
2. `/games-like/repo/`
3. `/features/repo-vs-lethal-company/`
4. `/games-like/lethal-company/`
5. `/games-like/content-warning/`
6. `/features/content-warning-vs-lethal-company/`
7. `/features/repo-less-scary-alternatives/`
8. `/games-like/phasmophobia/`
9. `/best/proximity-chat-horror-games/`
10. `/best/discord-night-games/`

## Success Markers By Day 14

- Homepage and core pages submitted in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- At least `10` core URLs manually requested for indexing
- At least `5` real third-party mentions or links live
- First impressions visible in Search Console
- Title and CTR review started from actual impression data, not guesswork

## Day 1

- Set up `Google Search Console`
- Set up `Bing Webmaster Tools`
- Submit `https://coopqueue.com/sitemap-index.xml`
- Verify homepage inspection result in both tools
- Create a working sheet with columns for URL, submitted date, indexed status, impressions, clicks, notes

## Day 2

- Request indexing for the homepage
- Request indexing for the first five core URLs
- Search Google with `site:coopqueue.com` and record what is already visible
- Check that canonicals, titles, and descriptions on the core URLs match the live intent

## Day 3

- Request indexing for the remaining five core URLs
- Add the same ten URLs into Bing URL submission
- Record which pages are still "Discovered" versus "Indexed"
- Do not change page copy yet unless something is obviously broken

## Day 4

- Start first-link outreach
- Reach out to friends, operators, or friendly sites for simple editorial mentions
- Look for small game blogs, personal blogs, and resource pages that can naturally link to one useful page
- Focus on relevance, not volume

## Day 5

- Post one or two useful community-native recommendations in relevant places
- Prioritize places where the page actually answers the question already being discussed
- Do not spray links
- Use the most relevant landing page, not always the homepage

## Day 6

- Review Search Console coverage and URL inspection again
- If pages are still not indexed, resubmit only the most important ones
- Check whether the homepage is linking strongly enough to the pushed pages
- Add notes on which URLs look slowest to get discovered

## Day 7

- Publish or secure another `2-3` real mentions or links
- Aim for links into:
  - one homepage or brand mention
  - one comparison page
  - one recommendation page
- Keep anchor text natural

## Day 8

- Review impressions for the first time
- Identify which pages are getting impressions without clicks
- Only then start title refinement for weak-CTR pages
- Leave pages with zero impressions alone unless they are still not indexed

## Day 9

- Improve homepage emphasis if one or two URLs are clearly becoming winners
- Keep the homepage linking to those winners in visible sections
- Make sure the best-performing comparison pages are easy to reach from other pages in the same cluster

## Day 10

- Decide monetization lane, but do not overbuild it yet
- Preferred order:
  1. affiliate or referral-style recommendations
  2. lightweight email capture if you have a real use for it
  3. ads later, after traffic exists
- Do not let monetization clutter the early trust-building phase

## Day 11

- Prepare monetization slots quietly
- Example:
  - one lightweight recommendation module near comparison conclusions
  - one soft CTA on high-intent pages
  - clear disclosure handling
- Do not launch aggressive placements yet

## Day 12

- Review internal linking again from actual early signals
- Strengthen links from broad pages into:
  - comparison winners
  - budget pages
  - party-size pages
  - less-scary / beginner routes

## Day 13

- Re-check coverage, impressions, clicks, and average position
- Separate URLs into:
  - indexed and moving
  - indexed but ignored
  - not indexed yet
- Only the third group deserves submission effort again

## Day 14

- Decide the next 14-day focus using data
- If impressions are arriving:
  - shift toward title and CTR refinement
  - add monetization modules softly on winner pages
- If impressions are still weak:
  - push another small batch of real mentions
  - review homepage prominence and internal linking before writing many new pages

## Link-Building Rules

- Prefer `5-10` real links over `50` weak directory links
- Link the page that best matches the referring context
- Do not buy garbage links
- Do not rely on random AI-generated outreach spam

## What Not To Do Right Now

- Do not publish dozens of thin pages
- Do not turn the site into an ad layout before traffic exists
- Do not keep rewriting every page without impression data
- Do not obsess over broad head terms before the constrained-intent pages move

## Best First Revenue Targets

When traffic starts arriving, the best early pages to monetize are:

1. `/features/repo-vs-lethal-company/`
2. `/features/content-warning-vs-lethal-company/`
3. `/features/repo-less-scary-alternatives/`
4. `/best/cheap-coop-horror-games-under-10/`
5. `/best/co-op-horror-games-for-beginners/`

These are the pages most likely to catch users close to a real install decision.
